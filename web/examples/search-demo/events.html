Events & Sponsorships

We're proud to know sponsors of the world's biggest events. Do they know we sponsor them? That's between us and our lawyers.

Super Bowl LXI

February 2027 • New Orleans, LA

We bought a 30-second ad slot and used the entire time to show a slowly rotating thinking token with no text, no voiceover, and no explanation. Marketing called it "aspirational minimalism." The internet called it "the worst Super Bowl ad of all time." Either way, people are talking about us, which is basically the same as revenue.

FIFA World Cup 2026

June–July 2026 • North America

Every player on every team received a complimentary Nano Thinker in their welcome bag. Several players were seen holding them during penalty shootouts, which we're counting as an endorsement. One goalkeeper claimed the token "distracted him" and caused a goal. Our legal team says that's actually great press.

2028 Olympic Games

July–August 2028 • Los Angeles, CA

We've petitioned the IOC to make "Competitive Thinking" an official Olympic sport. The petition was denied, but we did manage to get a Thinkify banner hung outside a parking garage three blocks from the main stadium. Close enough. We're also providing thinking tokens to all Olympic volunteers, because they deserve to think too.

ACL Cornhole Championship

August 2026 • Rock Hill, SC

This one we actually sponsor for real. The American Cornhole League Championship is the pinnacle of competitive bean bag tossing, and we believe it's the sport most aligned with our brand values: you stand still, you think about trajectory, and you throw a small object. That's basically what we sell. Our CEO is also weirdly good at cornhole, which may or may not have influenced this decision. Every competitor gets an Ultra Think Max (when back in stock).
